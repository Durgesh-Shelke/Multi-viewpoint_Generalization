## 2. Checkpoints Guide (`checkpoints/`)

### A. The Baseline Model
- **File:** `checkpoints/baseline_model_front_view_97.10percent.pt`
- **Architecture:** NatureCNN visual backbone ($128 \times 128 \times 3$ uint8 RGB) + 29-dim dynamic robot state MLP $\to 512$-dim latent $\to$ Gaussian Actor / Value Critic.
- **Trained on:** Front camera view (`eye = [0.300, 0.000, 0.600]`, `target = [-0.1, 0.0, 0.1]`).
- **Benchmark Performance:** **97.10% success** across 1,000 independent test episodes with randomized cube and target positions.

### B. Training Progression Checkpoints (`checkpoints/training_progression/`)
Checkpoints saved every 50 iterations (640,000 environment steps) during the initial training run:
- `ckpt_1.pt` (12.8k steps): Initial random policy (0.0% pick rate).
- `ckpt_51.pt` (652.8k steps): Exploring reach and table surface alignment (0.0%).
- `ckpt_101.pt` (1.29M steps): Learning approach vector towards the block (0.0%).
- `ckpt_151.pt` (1.93M steps): First successful grasp acquisitions (12.5% eval success).
- `ckpt_201.pt` (2.57M steps): Rapid policy convergence (87.5% eval success).
- `ckpt_251.pt` (3.21M steps): High-accuracy pickup (92.50% on 1,000 test runs).
- `ckpt_301.pt` to `ckpt_351.pt`: Refinement of gripper closure timing and vertical lift speed.
- `ckpt_401.pt` (5.13M steps): Final converged model (**97.10%** on 1,000 test runs).

### C. Sequential Viewpoint Checkpoints (`checkpoints/sequential_viewpoints_checkpoints/`)
Checkpoints generated by continually fine-tuning sequentially across the 30 Fibonacci equal-area viewpoints over ~15 hours:
- `ckpt_vp_00.pt`: Fine-tuned on Viewpoint 00 ($0^\circ, 12^\circ$). Achieved **86.0%–100.0%** on Viewpoint 00.
- `ckpt_vp_01.pt` to `ckpt_vp_28.pt`: Intermediate models fine-tuned across consecutive viewpoints.
- `ckpt_vp_29.pt`: Final model after sequential training across all 30 viewpoints.
- `viewpoints.json`: The exact spherical coordinates of all 30 Fibonacci viewpoints.
- `sequential_training_progress.json`: State history and iteration logs.

## 4. Key Scientific Insights from the Pure RGB Phase

1. **Severe Viewpoint Specificity:**
   Single-camera visual RL policies trained on 2D RGB pixels do not learn 3D world coordinates. Instead, they memorize visual coordinate mappings relative to the image boundaries. A shift of just $\pm 15^\circ$ in camera angle destroys policy execution.
2. **Failure of Continual Sequential Fine-Tuning:**
   Fine-tuning sequentially from one camera angle to another overwrites earlier convolutional filters. Even though the agent achieved $96\%\text{–}100\%$ on individual angles during their training phase, subsequent gradient updates erased those representations, causing complete catastrophic forgetting.
3. **The Necessity of RGB-D:**
   RGB alone forces the neural network to infer 3D depth from 2D visual perspective lines, which change completely when the camera moves. Metric depth channels ($[R, G, B, D]$) provide rotation-invariant 3D Euclidean distances, providing the geometric foundation required for true viewpoint-invariant manipulation.
